📘 UNIT 1 — Introduction

Objective, Scope and Outcome of the Course  |  Hours: 01

English

Analysis of Algorithms is the branch of computer science concerned with determining the amount of resources (time and space/memory) required by an algorithm to solve a problem, and with designing efficient algorithmic strategies (Divide & Conquer, Greedy, Dynamic Programming, Backtracking, Branch & Bound, Randomized) for solving computational problems optimally.

Hinglish

Analysis of Algorithms (AOA) ka matlab hai — kisi problem ko solve karne ke liye jo algorithm likha hai, woh kitna fast hai aur kitna memory use karta hai, yeh measure karna. Jaise do log ek hi problem solve karein — ek O(n²) approach use kare aur dusra O(n log n), toh dusra wala bahut fast hoga large data pe. Yeh subject sikhata hai ki best/optimal algorithm kaise design aur compare karte hain.

Objective of the Course High

  • To understand algorithm analysis fundamentals — time/space complexity, asymptotic notations (O, Ω, Θ).
  • To study Divide and Conquer strategy — Binary Search, Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Strassen's Matrix Multiplication.
  • To learn Greedy and Dynamic Programming approaches for optimization problems.
  • To understand Backtracking and Branch & Bound techniques for combinatorial search problems.
  • To study Pattern Matching algorithms (Naïve, KMP, Boyer-Moore, Rabin-Karp).
  • To explore Randomized Algorithms and the theory of NP-Completeness (P, NP, NP-Hard classes).
Hinglish

Course ka goal — tumhe sikhana ki ek problem ko solve karne ke multiple tareeke ho sakte hain, lekin sabka time/space cost alag hota hai. Tum seekhoge ki kis type ki problem mein kaunsi strategy use karni chahiye — greedy, DP, backtracking, ya branch and bound — aur kaise pata karein ki koi problem solve hi nahi ho sakti efficiently (NP-Complete).

Scope of the Course Medium

Analysis of Algorithms is the backbone of:

  • Software Engineering — Writing efficient code that scales for large inputs (Google, Amazon-scale systems).
  • Competitive Programming & Interviews — Core subject for technical interviews at top tech companies.
  • Database Systems — Query optimization, indexing algorithms.
  • Networking — Routing algorithms, shortest path, flow algorithms.
  • Cryptography & Security — Complexity theory underlies modern encryption (P vs NP).
  • AI/ML — Optimization algorithms, dynamic programming in reinforcement learning.
  • Operations Research — Assignment problems, scheduling, resource allocation.
Hinglish

Yeh subject sirf academic nahi hai — har tech company interview mein DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms) puchti hai, aur AOA hi uska theoretical foundation hai. Agar tumhe FAANG/product companies mein job chahiye, toh ye subject sabse important hai.

Outcome of the Course High

After completing this course, the student will be able to:

  • CO1: Analyze time and space complexity of algorithms using asymptotic notations.
  • CO2: Design and analyze Divide & Conquer algorithms (Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Strassen's algorithm).
  • CO3: Apply Greedy and Dynamic Programming techniques to solve optimization problems.
  • CO4: Apply Backtracking and Branch & Bound for combinatorial search problems (TSP, N-Queens).
  • CO5: Implement pattern matching algorithms and randomized algorithms.
  • CO6: Classify problems into P, NP, NP-Hard, NP-Complete and understand approximation algorithms.
Hinglish Trick

"Doctor Gave Dawaai Before Bed Night" (CO1-6: Divide & Conquer, Greedy, DP, Backtracking, Branch & Bound, NP-Complete) — AOA ke 6 core pillars ka sequence!

Unit 1 · Conceptual
Q3. Why is the study of algorithm efficiency important? ★★★ 5 Marks
5 Marks

As input size grows, an inefficient algorithm (e.g. O(n²)) can take impractically long compared to an efficient one (e.g. O(n log n)) — for n=10⁶, an O(n²) algorithm may take hours while an O(n log n) algorithm finishes in milliseconds. Efficiency analysis lets developers choose the right algorithm before implementation, avoiding costly performance problems at scale (large databases, real-time systems, big data).
