📘 UNIT 1 — Introduction

Objective, Scope and Outcome of the Course  |  Hours: 01

English

Information Theory & Coding (ITC) is the mathematical study of how information is quantified, encoded, transmitted, and decoded reliably and efficiently over noisy or noiseless communication channels and storage media.

Hinglish

Simple bhasha mein — ye subject batata hai ki "information" ko number mein kaise measure karein (entropy/bits), data ko compress kaise karein (kam space le), aur jab data noisy channel (jaise WiFi, satellite, CD/QR) se bheja jaaye to usme errors ko detect/correct kaise karein.

Objective of the Course High

  • To introduce the mathematical foundation of information measurement — uncertainty, entropy, mutual information.
  • To study source coding techniques (Huffman, Shannon-Fano, Lempel-Ziv) for data compaction.
  • To understand channel coding theorems and the limits of reliable communication (Shannon limit, channel capacity).
  • To design and analyze error-control codes: Linear Block Codes, Cyclic Codes, Convolutional Codes.
Hinglish

Course ka goal hai — tumhe ye samjhana ki kisi message mein "kitni naye baat / information" hai, use kaise efficiently bhejen (compression), aur agar transmission ke beech mein noise se error aa jaaye to use pakdo aur fix karo (error correcting codes — jo tumhare QR code, CD, satellite comm mein use hote hain).

Scope of the Course Medium

ITC forms the theoretical backbone of:

  • Data Compression — ZIP, JPEG, MP3, video codecs (entropy coding).
  • Reliable Digital Communication — mobile networks, satellite links, deep-space comm (Voyager used convolutional + Golay codes).
  • Storage Systems — CD/DVD/Hard-disk error correction (Reed-Solomon, cyclic codes).
  • Cryptography & Machine Learning — entropy concepts used in security and decision-tree/ML feature selection.
Hinglish

Ye subject sirf "theory" nahi — practically tumhare phone ke WiFi, 4G/5G, file .zip karna, QR code scan karna — sab ke peeche yahi concepts kaam karte hain. Isliye ye CS ka core fundamental subject hai, jaise DBMS ya OS.

Outcome of the Course High

After completing this course, the student will be able to:

  • CO1: Define and compute information, entropy, and mutual information for discrete/continuous sources.
  • CO2: Apply source coding theorem and design optimal codes (Huffman, Shannon-Fano).
  • CO3: Analyze discrete memoryless channels and compute channel capacity.
  • CO4: Design and decode Linear Block Codes and Cyclic Codes.
  • CO5: Understand Convolutional Codes and Viterbi decoding algorithm.

🗺️ Unit 1 Overview Map Quick Revise

UNIT 1: Communication Architecture & ITC Fundamentals

1. Information Quantification

  • Self Information: $I(x_i) = \log_2(1/p_i)$ bits
  • Entropy $H(X)$: Average uncertainty per symbol $\sum p_i \log(1/p_i)$
  • Mutual Information: Information shared between input & output

2. Source vs Channel Coding

  • Source Coding: Compression (Huffman, Shannon-Fano, LZ)
  • Channel Coding: Error correction (LBC, Cyclic, BCH, RS)
  • Shannon Capacity: Maximum error-free data rate $C$

3. Practical Applications

  • Storage: CD/DVD/HDD (Reed-Solomon, CRC)
  • Wireless: 4G/5G, WiFi, Satellite deep-space telemetry
  • Media: JPEG, MP3, ZIP archives, QR codes
