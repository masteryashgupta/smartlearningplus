Prim's Algorithm kya hai?

Socho tere paas kuch cities hain (jinhe "vertices" kehte hain), aur unke beech roads hain jinki apni-apni cost hai (jinhe "edges" kehte hain). Tujhe saari cities ko is tarah connect karna hai ki total cost sabse kam ho, aur koi bhi city chhoote nahi (sab connected rahein), aur koi cycle/loop bhi na bane. Isi final connected structure ko "Minimum Cost Spanning Tree (MST)" kehte hain.

Prim's Algorithm ka idea hai: kisi ek city se shuru karo (jaise city 0). Fir dekho ki abhi jitni cities connect ho chuki hain, unse jo agli SABSE SASTI road bahar ki kisi nayi city tak jaati hai — usi road ko chuno aur wo nayi city bhi apne "connected group" mein shaamil kar lo. Ye process tab tak repeat karo jab tak saari cities connect na ho jaayein.

Yaani Prim's Algorithm ek "growing tree" ki tarah kaam karta hai — dheere dheere ek hi group badhta jaata hai, har baar sabse sasti agli edge choose karke.


Chhota example:

5 cities (0 se 4), roads aur unki cost:
0-1 = 2, 0-3 = 6
1-2 = 3, 1-3 = 8, 1-4 = 5
2-4 = 7
3-4 = 9

City 0 se shuru karte hain (connected group = {0})

Step 1: {0} se bahar jaane wali sabse sasti road: 0-1 (cost=2)
        Group ab: {0, 1}

Step 2: {0,1} se bahar jaane wali sabse sasti road: 1-2 (cost=3)
        Group ab: {0, 1, 2}

Step 3: {0,1,2} se bahar jaane wali sabse sasti road: 1-4 (cost=5)
        Group ab: {0, 1, 2, 4}

Step 4: {0,1,2,4} se bahar jaane wali sabse sasti road: 0-3 (cost=6)
        Group ab: {0, 1, 2, 3, 4}   -> sab cities connect ho gayi!

Total minimum cost = 2+3+5+6 = 16


Speed ka mamla:

Simple adjacency matrix wale implementation (jo is program mein use hua hai) mein Prim's Algorithm O(n^2) time leta hai, jahan n cities ki sankhya hai. Bade graphs ke liye is se fast (priority queue wali) versions bhi hoti hain, lekin ye simple version chhote-medium graphs ke liye theek hai.


Tera program kya karta hai:

1. Tujhse pucha jaata hai kitni vertices (cities) hain.
2. Tujhse pura cost matrix enter karwaya jaata hai (kaunsi city se kaunsi city tak kitni cost hai; 0 matlab koi road nahi).
3. Program city 0 se shuru karke, har baar connected group se bahar jaane wali sabse sasti edge dhundta hai aur use MST mein add kar leta hai (upar wala process).
4. Har chuni gayi edge print hoti hai, saath uski cost bhi.
5. Last mein total minimum cost print hota hai.
6. Calculation mein kitna time laga, wo bhi dikhaya jaata hai.

Bas itna hi — ek city se shuru karo, har baar apne connected group se bahar jaane wali sabse sasti agli road pakdo, jab tak sab connect na ho jaayein.
