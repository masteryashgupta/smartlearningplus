Quick Sort kya hai?

Socho tere paas ek array hai jisme random numbers hain, jaise [10, 7, 8, 9, 1, 5]. Tujhe inko chhote se bade order mein arrange karna hai.

Quick Sort ka idea simple hai — ek number ko "pivot" bana lo (usually last wala), aur baaki sab numbers ko do groups mein baant do:
- Jo pivot se chhote hain -> left side
- Jo pivot se bade hain -> right side

Bas itna karne se pivot apni final sahi jagah pe pahunch jaata hai (kyunki uske left mein sab chhote hain aur right mein sab bade). Ab yahi kaam tu left group pe bhi karta hai aur right group pe bhi — same trick baar baar. Jab tak koi group sirf 1 ya 0 number ka na reh jaaye (matlab wo already sorted hai), tab tak ye process chalta rehta hai.


Chhota example:

[10, 7, 8, 9, 1, 5]   pivot = 5

Chhote wale (1) left mein, bade wale (10,7,8,9) right mein:
[1] 5 [10, 7, 8, 9]

Ab 5 apni sahi jagah pe fix ho gaya. Left group mein sirf 1 hai, wo already sorted.
Right group [10,7,8,9] pe wahi process phir se:
pivot = 9 -> [10,7,8] 9 []

phir [10,7,8] pe: pivot = 8 -> [7] 8 [10]

Sab jodke: [1, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10]  -> sorted!


Speed ka mamla:

Zyada tar cases mein Quick Sort kaafi fast hota hai (O(n log n)) — matlab bade array pe bhi jaldi sort ho jaata hai. Lekin agar bahut unlucky ho (jaise array pehle se sorted ho aur pivot hamesha sabse chhota/bada element ban jaaye), tab ye slow ho sakta hai (O(n^2)) — kyunki har baar sirf 1 element hi alag hota hai, groups barabar nahi banate.


Tera program kya karta hai:

1. Pehle tujhse pucha jaata hai kitne numbers sort karne hain.
2. Random numbers generate hote hain (0-999 ke beech), unhe "Unsorted Array" ke naam se dikhaya jaata hai.
3. Fir Quick Sort chalta hai (jo upar wala process hai), aur timer bhi start hota hai.
4. Sort hone ke baad "Sorted Array" print hota hai.
5. Aur sorting mein kitna time laga (seconds mein), wo bhi dikhaya jaata hai.

Bas itna hi — pivot chuno, chhote-bade baanto, repeat karo jab tak sab sorted na ho jaaye.
