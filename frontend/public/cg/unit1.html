📘 UNIT 1 — Introduction

Objective, Scope and Outcome of the Course  |  Hours: 01

English

Computer Graphics is the field of computer science concerned with generating, manipulating, storing, and displaying visual images and geometric data using computers. It encompasses the theory and practice of creating 2D and 3D images, animations, and visual simulations — from line drawings to photorealistic rendering.

Hinglish

Computer Graphics ka matlab — computer use karke visual cheezein banana (images, diagrams, animations, games, movies). Jab tum koi photo edit karte ho (Photoshop), game khelate ho (PUBG, GTA), ya movie dekhte ho jisme CGI hai (Avengers) — ye sab Computer Graphics ki den hai. Ye subject batata hai ki in sab ke peeche ka mathematics aur algorithms kaise kaam karte hain.

Objective of the Course High

  • To understand the fundamentals of computer graphics — display hardware, coordinate systems, and rendering pipeline.
  • To study scan conversion algorithms for basic graphics primitives (lines, circles, ellipses, polygons).
  • To learn 2D and 3D geometric transformations — translation, rotation, scaling, reflection, shearing.
  • To understand clipping algorithms (Cohen-Sutherland, Liang-Barsky) and the window-to-viewport mapping.
  • To explore 3D representation, projection, illumination models, and colour models (RGB, HSV, CMY).
  • To introduce animation techniques, fractals, ray tracing, and realism in computer graphics.
Hinglish

Course ka goal — tumhe computer pe visuals draw karne ke peeche ka math samjhana: screen pe ek line draw karte hain to exactly kaun se pixels colour hote hain (scan conversion)? Object ko rotate karna ho to matrix multiplication kaise kaam karta hai? 3D object ko 2D screen pe project kaise karte hain? Light aur colour ka math kya hai? In sab sawaalon ka systematic jawab yahi subject deta hai.

Scope of the Course Medium

Computer Graphics is the backbone of:

  • Gaming Industry — Real-time 3D rendering engines (Unreal Engine, Unity) — biggest application.
  • Film & VFX — CGI, motion capture, special effects (Pixar, Marvel Studios).
  • CAD/CAM — Engineering design tools (AutoCAD, SolidWorks) for mechanical/civil design.
  • Medical Imaging — MRI/CT scan visualization, surgical simulators.
  • UI/UX Design — Every app/website UI you see involves 2D graphics rendering.
  • Data Visualization — Charts, maps, scientific simulations (weather models, fluid dynamics).
  • Virtual & Augmented Reality — VR headsets, AR overlays, metaverse.
Hinglish

Ye subject sirf theory nahi — gaming, movies, medical, architecture, AR/VR har jagah CG hai. Isiliye ye ek evergreen high-demand field hai. India mein bhi gaming industry rapidly grow kar rahi hai aur CG engineers ki demand badh rahi hai.

Outcome of the Course High

After completing this course, the student will be able to:

  • CO1: Explain CG fundamentals — display hardware, scan systems, graphics pipeline.
  • CO2: Implement scan conversion algorithms for lines (DDA, Bresenham), circles, and ellipses.
  • CO3: Apply 2D/3D geometric transformations using matrix and homogeneous coordinates.
  • CO4: Apply clipping algorithms (Cohen-Sutherland, Liang-Barsky) and window-to-viewport mapping.
  • CO5: Understand 3D viewing pipeline, projection transformations, and Bezier/B-spline curves.
  • CO6: Explain illumination models, colour models (RGB/HSV/CMY), and animation techniques.

Unit 1 · Conceptual
Q3. What is the role of GPU in computer graphics? Explain the graphics pipeline. ★★★ 5 Marks
5 Marks

GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) is a specialized processor for rendering images. The pipeline: Application Program → Graphics API (OpenGL/DirectX) → GPU (vertex processing, rasterization, fragment shading) → Frame Buffer → Display. GPU excels at massively parallel floating-point operations needed for pixel computation.
